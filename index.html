Privacy Policy for Spell Out (formerly Roll & Spell)

Effective Date: 16 October 2026

Introduction

Spell Out (formerly Roll & Spell) is developed by Stone Turtle Games. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We don't ask for personal details such as your name, email address or phone number. To run the game's online features and to improve the game, we use the data described below.

Data the Game Uses

  • Player ID: when you first open the game, Unity Gaming Services creates an anonymous player ID for you. Your online games and scores are linked to it.
  • In-game name: the game gives you a random name, for example "SunnyOtter#4823". Other players can see it in online matches, on leaderboards and in notifications.
  • Game data: your online matches (the words you play, scores and results), your online stats and your leaderboard scores, stored on Unity's servers.
  • Usage data: we use Unity Analytics to see how the game is played, for example game starts and ends, tutorial progress, menu use and ad events. We use it to fix problems and improve the game. It doesn't include your name or contact details.
  • Notification ID: if you play online, the game registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, so we can tell you when it's your move or a match has finished. This uses a Firebase installation ID, which we store with your online game data. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Third-Party Services

Spell Out uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – used to display ads
  • Google Play Services – required for app distribution and functionality
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Analytics), by Unity Technologies: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, by Google: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (through Unity IAP), used to process the full-version purchase. We don't see or store your payment details.

We don't use Firebase for analytics or advertising. Unity and Google may process data outside the UK.

These services may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Learn more about Google’s advertising policies here: Google Privacy & Terms – Advertising

Advertising & Consent

Users in the EU, UK, and California are shown a consent form using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), allowing them to choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.

Manage or Revoke Consent

You can manage or revoke your advertising consent at any time via the in-app Privacy Settings option, which reopens the consent form.

Children’s Privacy

Spell Out does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that such information has been collected, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your online game data while you use the game's online features. A notification ID is removed when it stops working. To ask us to delete your data, email stoneturtlegames@gmail.com. If you know your full in-game name with its # number (for example SunnyOtter#4823), include it; it appears on the leaderboard next to "(YOU)". If you can't find it, email us anyway and we'll help you. We will delete your player account and its online data within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone, but not your online data.

Data Security

Your data is stored by Unity and Google, who protect them with their own security measures, and it is sent over encrypted connections.
