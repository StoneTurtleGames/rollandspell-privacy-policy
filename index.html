Privacy Policy for Roll & Spell

Roll & Spell is developed by Stone Turtle Games. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Personal Data

Stone Turtle Games does not collect or store any personal data directly. You are not required to provide personal information to use Roll & Spell.

Usage Data

We do not collect usage analytics or tracking data ourselves. However, third-party services used by the app may collect limited data as described below.

Third-Party Services

Roll & Spell uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – used to display ads

These services may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Learn more about Google’s advertising policies here: Google Privacy & Terms – Advertising

Advertising & Consent

Users in the EU, UK, and California are shown a consent form using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), allowing them to choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.

Manage or Revoke Consent

You can manage or revoke your advertising consent at any time via the in-app Privacy Settings option, which reopens the consent form.

Children’s Privacy

Roll & Spell does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that such information has been collected, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Security

We do not store personal data. Any data processed by third-party services is handled according to their own security practices.
